    biology/epidemiology

    agent_based_sir

    People who walk on a grid and infect those in their cell, with optional extensions of the SIR model.

    • Field: biology/epidemiology
    • Formalism: agent-based model
    • References: Argun et al. 2021; Kermack and McKendrick 1927

    Example

    Factory

    @register_model(
    field='biology/epidemiology',
    tags=('compartments', 'agents', 'grid', 'lockdown', 'mortality'),
    references=('Argun et al. 2021', 'Kermack and McKendrick 1927'),
    defaults={'until': 300},
)
def agent_based_sir(
    width: int = 50,
    height: int = 50,
    *,
    people: int = 500,
    infected: int = 5,
    d: float = 0.8,
    beta: float = 0.6,
    gamma: float = 0.02,
    mu: float = 0.0,
    alpha: float = 0.0,
    incubation: float = 0.0,
    lockdown_start: float = 0.0,
    lockdown_length: float = 0.0,
    lockdown_d: float = 0.1,
    random_state: int | np.random.Generator | None = 0,
) -> System:
    """Return people who walk on a grid and infect those in their cell, with optional extensions of the SIR model.

    The basic model infects with probability `beta` and recovers with probability `gamma`. Its final size depends on
    `beta` and `gamma` separately, not only on their ratio, because walking limits the contacts. Mortality `mu`, waning
    immunity `alpha`, an incubation of `incubation` steps, and a lockdown that lowers the probability of moving to
    `lockdown_d` extend it.

    Args:
        width:
            The number of columns of the grid.

        height:
            The number of rows of the grid.

        people:
            The number of people.

        infected:
            The initial number of infected people.

        d:
            The probability of moving at every step.

        beta:
            The probability that an infectious person infects the susceptible persons of its cell.

        gamma:
            The probability of recovering at every step.

        mu:
            The probability of dying at every step of the infection.

        alpha:
            The probability of losing immunity at every step.

        incubation:
            The number of steps an infected person stays exposed before it becomes infectious, counting the step of
            the exposure, as the residence time of `TransitionInteraction` counts them.

        lockdown_start:
            The step at which the lockdown starts.

        lockdown_length:
            The number of steps of the lockdown, 0 for none.

        lockdown_d:
            The probability of moving during the lockdown.

        random_state:
            The seed or generator of the initial cells and infections.

    Returns:
        system:
            The model, with the group `people`.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.biology.epidemiology import agent_based_sir
        >>> int(agent_based_sir(people=100, infected=3, random_state=0).elements[0].values['health'].sum())
        6
    """
    rng = np.random.default_rng(random_state)
    cells = rng.integers(0, [width, height], size=(people, 2))
    group = GroupElement(
        'people',
        element=PersonElement,
        size=people,
        values={
            'health': _initial_health(people, infected, rng, INFECTED),
            'd': d,
            'beta': beta,
            'gamma': gamma,
            'mu': mu,
            'alpha': alpha,
            'lockdown_start': lockdown_start,
            'lockdown_length': lockdown_length,
            'lockdown_d': lockdown_d,
        },
        location=[tuple(int(item) for item in cell) for cell in cells],
    )
    walk = MovementInteraction('walk', target='people', condition=_is_living_person, accept=_get_person_step)
    space = LatticeSpace((width, height), boundary='fixed', neighborhood='von_neumann')
    return System(elements=[group], interactions=[walk, *_get_person_transitions(incubation)], space=space)

    Components

    class PersonElement(Element):
    """Person of the agent-based SIR model, with incubation, mortality, waning immunity, and lockdown.

    A `MovementInteraction` walks a living person to a neighbouring cell with probability `d`, or `lockdown_d` during
    the lockdown, which lasts `lockdown_length` steps from `lockdown_start`. Transitions of the agent form, which
    `agent_based_sir` adds, move the health of the person. An infectious person infects, with probability `beta`,
    every susceptible person in its cell, who becomes exposed. An infectious person recovers with probability `gamma`
    or dies with probability `mu`, and a recovered person loses immunity with probability `alpha`. Health is 0
    susceptible, 1 exposed, 2 infected, 3 recovered, or 4 dead.

    The incubation follows the convention of the residence time `after` of `TransitionInteraction`: the step of the
    exposure counts as the first step in the exposed state, so a person exposed in step `n` becomes infectious at the
    end of step `n + incubation - 1`. The clock that the person kept before counted the step of the exposure only when
    the person acted after its infector in that step, so the onset now comes half a step earlier on average.

    Args:
        label:
            The label of the agent or group.

        visual:
            How the element is drawn, or `None` for `default_visual_`.

        health:
            The health state.

        d:
            The probability of moving at every step.

        beta:
            The probability that an infectious person infects the susceptible persons of its cell.

        gamma:
            The probability of recovering at every step.

        mu:
            The probability of dying at every step of the infection.

        alpha:
            The probability of losing immunity at every step.

        lockdown_start:
            The step at which the lockdown starts.

        lockdown_length:
            The number of steps of the lockdown, 0 for none.

        lockdown_d:
            The probability of moving during the lockdown.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.core.elements import GroupElement
        >>> from skcomplex.models.biology.epidemiology import PersonElement
        >>> GroupElement('people', element=PersonElement, size=100).size
        100
    """

    def __init__(
        self,
        label: str,
        visual: VisualSpec | None = VisualSpec('agents', {'color_by': 'health', 'color_range': (0.0, 4.0)}),
        *,
        health: Annotated[float, StateSpec(domain=NN)] = SUSCEPTIBLE,
        d: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed_open(0, oo))] = 0.8,
        beta: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed_open(0, oo))] = 0.6,
        gamma: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed_open(0, oo))] = 0.01,
        mu: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed_open(0, oo))] = 0.0,
        alpha: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed_open(0, oo))] = 0.0,
        lockdown_start: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed_open(0, oo))] = 0.0,
        lockdown_length: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed_open(0, oo))] = 0.0,
        lockdown_d: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed_open(0, oo))] = 0.1,
    ) -> None:
        super().__init__(label, visual=visual)
        self.health = health
        self.d = d
        self.beta = beta
        self.gamma = gamma
        self.mu = mu
        self.alpha = alpha
        self.lockdown_start = lockdown_start
        self.lockdown_length = lockdown_length
        self.lockdown_d = lockdown_d

    @classmethod
    def _get_view(cls) -> type[PersonView]:
        """Return the view of a person, `PersonView`."""
        return PersonView

class MovementInteraction(Interaction):
    """Movement of the agents of a group by steps, every step of discrete time.

    A step is one of five kinds:

    - `'neighbor'` moves an agent to a uniformly drawn cell or node next to its own, on a lattice or a network.
    - `'gaussian'` adds to the position of an agent a normal vector whose components have the standard deviation
      `scale`, in a continuous space.
    - `'levy'` moves an agent in a uniformly drawn direction by a Pareto length, whose least value is `scale` and whose
      tail exponent is `exponent`, in a continuous space.
    - `'best'` moves an agent to the cell or node next to its own with the highest `score(agent, cell, model)`. With a
      `vision`, a number or a function of the agent and the model view, it looks instead along every direction of the
      neighbourhood of a lattice up to that many cells, and at the cell of the agent, in the order of the axes, the
      positive direction first. Among cells of equal highest score, the ties `'random'` draw one uniformly, `'first'`
      take the first in that order, and `'nearest'` take the nearest, the first of them among equal distances. By
      default the ties are `'random'` without a vision and `'nearest'` with one.
    - A function `step(agent, model)` returns the destination, or `None` to stay.

    An agent for which `condition(agent, model)` holds attempts one step every step of time, or a Poisson number of
    steps with the mean `rate`, and takes each attempt with the probability `probability` and then with the probability
    `accept(agent, destination, model)`. An exclusive movement never moves an agent to a cell another agent of the group
    holds. The agents move one after the other, in their order under the schedule `'sequential'`, in a new random order
    every step under `'random-sequential'`, or each in its own activation under `'member'`, together with the rules of
    its element and the other member interactions, in the order the system lists them. Every step taken charges its
    `cost`, a `TransferInteraction` from the agent to itself or a function of the agent and the model view. The
    movement is an agent rule, so the system runs in discrete time.

    Args:
        label:
            The label of the interaction, unique among the parts of its system.

        target:
            The path of the group whose agents move.

        step:
            The kind of step, `'neighbor'`, `'gaussian'`, `'levy'`, or `'best'`, or a function of the agent and the
            model view that returns the destination.

        probability:
            The probability that an agent takes an attempted step.

        rate:
            The mean number of attempted steps of an agent in one unit of time, or `None` for one attempt every step.

        condition:
            A function of the agent and the model view that returns whether the agent moves, or `None` for every agent.

        accept:
            A function of the agent, its destination, and the model view that returns the probability of taking the
            step, or `None` to take every step.

        exclusive:
            Whether an agent moves only to cells or nodes no other agent of the group holds.

        score:
            The function of the agent, a neighbouring cell, and the model view that the step `'best'` maximizes.

        scale:
            The standard deviation of every component of a Gaussian step, or the least length of a Lévy step.

        exponent:
            The tail exponent of the length of a Lévy step.

        cost:
            A `TransferInteraction` that the agent pays to itself or to the environment for every step taken, a function
            of the agent and the model view run for every step taken, or `None`.

        vision:
            The number of cells the step `'best'` looks along every direction of a lattice, a number or a function of
            the agent and the model view, or `None` for the cells next to the agent.

        schedule:
            The order of the agents, `'sequential'`, `'random-sequential'`, or `'member'`.

        phase:
            The phase of the step the movement runs in, or `None` for the default phase.

        ties:
            The cell the step `'best'` takes among cells of equal highest score, `'random'`, `'first'`, or `'nearest'`,
            or `None` for `'random'` without a vision and `'nearest'` with one.

    Raises:
        ValidationError:
            The step, the schedule, or the ties are unknown, the rate is not positive, a function is not callable, or
            the step `'best'` has no score, raised by `check`.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.core.elements import Element, GroupElement
        >>> from skcomplex.core.interactions import MovementInteraction
        >>> from skcomplex.core.spaces import LatticeSpace
        >>> from skcomplex.core.system import System
        >>> from skcomplex.engine.runtime import Simulation
        >>> walkers = GroupElement('walkers', element=Element, size=2, location=[(2, 2), (2, 2)])
        >>> walk = MovementInteraction('walk', target='walkers', step='neighbor')
        >>> space = LatticeSpace((5, 5), neighborhood='von_neumann')
        >>> system = System(elements=[walkers], interactions=[walk], space=space)
        >>> trajectory = Simulation(system, random_state=0).check().prepare().run(until=1).trajectory_
        >>> [bool(abs(cell - 2).sum() == 1) for cell in trajectory.agents('walkers', 1).locations]
        [True, True]
    """

    def __init__(
        self,
        label: str,
        *,
        target: Annotated[str, RoleSpec()],
        step: Step = 'neighbor',
        probability: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.closed(0, 1))] = 1.0,
        rate: float | None = None,
        condition: Callable[[AgentViewProtocol, ModelViewProtocol], bool] | None = None,
        accept: Callable[[AgentViewProtocol, object, ModelViewProtocol], float] | None = None,
        exclusive: bool = False,
        score: Callable[[AgentViewProtocol, object, ModelViewProtocol], float] | None = None,
        scale: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.open(0, oo))] = 1.0,
        exponent: Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=RealSet.open(0, oo))] = 1.5,
        cost: Cost | None = None,
        vision: Vision | None = None,
        schedule: str = 'random-sequential',
        phase: str | None = None,
        ties: str | None = None,
    ) -> None:
        super().__init__(label)
        self.target = target
        self.step = step
        self.probability = probability
        self.rate = rate
        self.condition = condition
        self.accept = accept
        self.exclusive = exclusive
        self.score = score
        self.scale = scale
        self.exponent = exponent
        self.cost = cost
        self.vision = vision
        self.schedule = schedule
        self.phase = phase
        self.ties = ties

    def _check_functions(self) -> None:
        """Raise an error for a condition, an acceptance, a score, or a cost that cannot be called."""
        for name in ('condition', 'accept', 'score'):
            value = getattr(self, name)
            if value is not None and not callable(value):
                error_msg = f'The {name} of {self.label!r} is {value!r}. Give a function, or None.'
                raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if isinstance(self.cost, TransferInteraction):
            self.cost.check()
        elif self.cost is not None and not callable(self.cost):
            error_msg = f'The cost of {self.label!r} is {self.cost!r}. Give a TransferInteraction, a function, or None.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)

    def _check_space(self, space: BaseSpace | None) -> None:
        """Raise an error for a step to a neighbouring cell or node in a continuous space."""
        if isinstance(self.step, str) and self.step in GRID_STEPS:
            self._check_grid('step', self.step, space, "step='gaussian' or step='levy', or a step function")

    def _check(self) -> None:
        """Raise an error for an unknown step, a rate that is not positive, or a function that cannot be called.

        Raises:
            ValidationError:
                The step, the schedule, or the ties are unknown, the rate is not positive, a function is not callable,
                or the step `'best'` has no score.
        """
        super()._check()
        self._check_variant('step', self.step, STEPS, functions=True)
        if self.rate is not None and (isinstance(self.rate, bool) or not self.rate > 0):
            error_msg = f'The rate of {self.label!r} is {self.rate!r}. Give a positive number, or None.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if self.schedule not in SCHEDULES:
            error_msg = f'Unknown schedule {self.schedule!r} of {self.label!r}. Use one of {", ".join(SCHEDULES)}.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if self.ties is not None:
            self._check_variant('ties', self.ties, TIES)
        if self.vision is not None and not callable(self.vision) and not isinstance(self.vision, int | float):
            error_msg = f'The vision of {self.label!r} is {self.vision!r}. Give a number, a function, or None.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if self.step == 'best' and self.score is None:
            error_msg = f'The step `best` of {self.label!r} maximizes a score. Pass `score(agent, cell, model)`.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        self._check_functions()

    def _count_attempts(self, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> int:
        """Return the number of steps an agent attempts in one step of time."""
        return 1 if self.rate is None else int(model.rng.poisson(self.rate))

    def _get_cells(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, occupied: set[object]) -> list[object]:
        """Return the cells or nodes around an agent that it may move to."""
        cells = agent.neighbor_cells()
        if not self.exclusive:
            return cells
        return [cell for cell in cells if cell not in occupied]

    def _get_best(
        self,
        agent: AgentViewProtocol,
        cells: list[object],
        model: ModelViewProtocol,
        distances: list[int] | None = None,
    ) -> object:
        """Return the cell of the highest score, chosen among ties by the tie rule."""
        if self.score is None or not cells:
            return None
        scores = [self.score(agent, cell, model) for cell in cells]
        ties = self.ties or ('random' if distances is None else 'nearest')
        if ties != 'nearest':
            return cells[self._pick_best(scores, ties, model)]
        values = np.asarray(scores, dtype=float)
        best = np.flatnonzero(values == values.max())
        near = np.asarray([1 if distances is None else distances[int(index)] for index in best])
        return cells[int(best[near == near.min()][0])]

    @staticmethod
    def _shift(here: tuple[int, ...], cell: tuple[int, ...], model: ModelViewProtocol) -> tuple[int, ...]:
        """Return the unit step from a cell to a neighbouring cell, across the wrapped edge of a torus."""
        shift = [b - a for a, b in zip(here, cell, strict=True)]
        if model.torus:
            sizes = [int(item) for item in model.dimensions or ()]
            shift = [d - round(d / n) * n for d, n in zip(shift, sizes, strict=True)]
        return tuple(shift)

    def _get_sight(
        self,
        agent: AgentViewProtocol,
        occupied: set[object],
        model: ModelViewProtocol,
    ) -> tuple[list[object], list[int]]:
        """Return the cells in sight of an agent along every direction of a lattice, its own first, with distances."""
        vision = self.vision(agent, model) if callable(self.vision) else self.vision
        reach = int(vision or 0)
        here = tuple(int(item) for item in np.atleast_1d(np.asarray(agent.location_key)))
        sizes = [int(item) for item in model.dimensions or ()]
        cells: list[object] = [agent.location_key]
        distances = [0]
        shifts = [
            self._shift(here, tuple(int(item) for item in np.atleast_1d(np.asarray(neighbor))), model)
            for neighbor in agent.neighbor_cells()
        ]
        for shift in sorted(shifts, key=lambda step: tuple((item == 0, -item) for item in step)):
            for distance in range(1, reach + 1):
                point = [a + distance * d for a, d in zip(here, shift, strict=True)]
                if model.torus:
                    point = [item % size for item, size in zip(point, sizes, strict=True)]
                elif any(not 0 <= item < size for item, size in zip(point, sizes, strict=True)):
                    break
                cell = tuple(point)
                if not self.exclusive or cell not in occupied:
                    cells.append(cell)
                    distances.append(distance)
        return cells, distances

    def _get_destination(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, model: ModelViewProtocol, occupied: set[object]) -> object:
        """Return the location a step of an agent leads to, or `None` to stay."""
        rng = model.rng
        if callable(self.step):
            return self.step(agent, model)
        if self.step == 'best' and self.vision is not None:
            cells, distances = self._get_sight(agent, occupied, model)
            return self._get_best(agent, cells, model, distances)
        if self.step in ('neighbor', 'best'):
            cells = self._get_cells(agent, occupied)
            if self.step == 'best':
                return self._get_best(agent, cells, model)
            return cells[int(rng.integers(len(cells)))] if cells else None
        position = np.asarray(agent.location, dtype=float)
        if self.step == 'gaussian':
            return position + rng.normal(0.0, self._get_parameter_value('scale', model), position.shape)
        direction = rng.normal(size=position.shape)
        length = self._get_parameter_value('scale', model) * (
            1.0 + rng.pareto(self._get_parameter_value('exponent', model))
        )
        return position + length * direction / np.linalg.norm(direction)

    def _charge(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> None:
        """Charge the cost of one step taken to an agent."""
        if isinstance(self.cost, TransferInteraction):
            self.cost.transfer(agent, agent, model)
        elif self.cost is not None:
            self.cost(agent, model)

    def _take(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, destination: object, occupied: set[object]) -> bool:
        """Move an agent to a destination unless an exclusive movement finds it held, and return whether it moved."""
        if not self.exclusive:
            agent.move_to(destination)
            return True
        if destination == agent.location_key:
            return True
        if destination in occupied:
            return False
        occupied.discard(agent.location_key)
        agent.move_to(destination)
        occupied.add(agent.location_key)
        return True

    def _move(
        self,
        agent: AgentViewProtocol,
        model: ModelViewProtocol,
        probability: float,
        occupied: set[object],
    ) -> None:
        """Take the attempted steps of one agent, each with its probability and acceptance, charging every step."""
        for _ in range(self._count_attempts(model)):
            if probability < 1.0 and model.rng.random() >= probability:
                continue
            destination = self._get_destination(agent, model, occupied)
            if destination is None:
                continue
            if self.accept is not None and model.rng.random() >= self.accept(agent, destination, model):
                continue
            if self._take(agent, destination, occupied):
                self._charge(agent, model)

    def _move_member(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> None:
        """Move one agent whose condition holds, in its own activation."""
        if agent.alive and (self.condition is None or self.condition(agent, model)):
            occupied = (
                {other.location_key for other in model.agents(self._get_role('target'))} if self.exclusive else set()
            )
            self._move(agent, model, self._get_parameter_value('probability', model), occupied)

    def _move_all(self, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> None:
        """Move every agent of the group whose condition holds, in their order or in random order."""
        agents = model.agents(self._get_role('target'))
        occupied = {agent.location_key for agent in agents} if self.exclusive else set()
        probability = self._get_parameter_value('probability', model)
        order = model.rng.permutation(len(agents)) if self.schedule == 'random-sequential' else range(len(agents))
        for index in order:
            agent = agents[int(index)]
            if agent.alive and (self.condition is None or self.condition(agent, model)):
                self._move(agent, model, probability, occupied)

    def _get_laws(self, namespace: SimpleNamespace) -> list[BaseLaw]:
        """Return the rule of the movement.

        Args:
            namespace:
                The namespace of the symbols the laws can use.

        Returns:
            laws:
                The rule that moves the agents every step.
        """
        if self.schedule == 'member':
            return [RuleLaw(self._move_member, phase=self.phase, members=self._get_role('target'))]
        return [RuleLaw(self._move_all, phase=self.phase)]

class TransitionInteraction(Interaction):
    """Move of a discrete state of the target from one value to another, or between all its values by a matrix.

    A member whose state holds `source` moves to `destination` at a `rate`, as a jump in continuous time, with a
    `probability` in every step of discrete time, or in the step in which a `condition` holds. With `over='neighbors'`
    the rate and the probability are those of one neighbour, written with `namespace.target.other` for the neighbour:
    the rates of the neighbours add up, and their probabilities combine as `1 - prod(1 - p)`, the probability that at
    least one of them moves the member. With a matrix instead, the state takes the values `0` to `n - 1` and steps by
    the rows of a transition matrix in discrete time, the row of its current value. An `emission` matrix then draws the
    `observed` state of the target from the row that the next value of the state selects, as a hidden Markov source.
    With a residence time `after`, a member moves by a probability or a condition only once it has held the source
    value for `after` steps, at the earliest at the end of the step that completes them.

    In the `'symbolic'` form, every function reads `namespace.target`, the states and parameters of the target, and the
    time `namespace.t`. In the `'agent'` form, the target is an agent group and every function takes the agent and
    the model view, `f(agent, model)`, and returns a number, a truth value, or the rows of a matrix, and a rate `r`
    moves an agent with probability `1 - exp(-r)` in a step. With `over='cell'`, every member acts on the other members
    of its cell: it moves each one that holds the source value with the probability, the rate, or the condition
    `f(agent, other, model)` of that member `agent` and the acting member `other`, each by its own draw.

    The `schedule` of the agent form orders the members. Under `'synchronous'` every member decides from the states at
    the start of the step, under `'sequential'` the members act one after the other in their order, and under
    `'random-sequential'` in a new random order every step, each reading the values the members before it set. Under
    `'member'` every member acts in its own activation, together with the rules of its element and the other member
    interactions, in the order the system lists them, so one rule of a member runs its movement, transfers,
    transitions, and life cycle.

    Args:
        label:
            The label of the interaction, unique among the parts of its system.

        target:
            The path of the element or group whose state moves.

        state:
            The name of the discrete state of the target.

        source:
            The value the state moves from, a collection of such values, or a function of the value of the state that
            returns whether the state moves from it, such as `lambda value: value >= 0`, a condition in the symbolic
            form. A state that moves from several values moves to the destination from each.

        destination:
            The value the state moves to.

        rate:
            The function of the rate of the move, or `None`.

        probability:
            The function of the probability of the move in a step, or `None`.

        condition:
            The function of the condition under which the state moves in a step, or `None`.

        matrix:
            The function of the transition matrix, one row and one column per value of the state, or `None`.

        emission:
            With a matrix, the function of the emission matrix, one row per value of the state and one column per value
            of the observed state, or `None`.

        observed:
            The name of the state of the target that the emission matrix draws, or `None`.

        over:
            `'member'` for a rate or a probability of the member, `'neighbors'` for that of one neighbour, or `'cell'`
            for the agent form whose members act on the members of their cell.

        neighborhood:
            The neighbourhood of `over='neighbors'`, or `None` for the neighbours of the network or the lattice.

        after:
            The number of steps a member holds the source value before it can move, or `None`.

        schedule:
            The order of the members of the agent form, `'synchronous'`, `'sequential'`, `'random-sequential'`, or
            `'member'`.

        form:
            `'symbolic'` for laws of the states of the target, or `'agent'` for a rule over an agent group.

        phase:
            The phase of the step the agent form runs in, or `None` for the default phase.

    Raises:
        ValidationError:
            The transition has not exactly one kind, the source and the destination are equal, an emission has no
            matrix or no observed state, `over` is unknown or goes with a form or a kind it cannot take, the
            residence time is negative or goes with a rate, a matrix, or members acting on their cell, the form or the
            schedule is unknown, the symbolic form has a schedule, or a function is not callable.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.core.interactions import TransitionInteraction
        >>> recovery = TransitionInteraction(
        ...     'recovery', target='hosts', state='state', source=1, destination=0, rate=lambda s: 0.5
        ... )
        >>> recovery.check().over
        'member'
        >>> agents = TransitionInteraction(
        ...     'recovery', target='hosts', state='state', source=1, destination=2, probability=lambda a, m: 0.1,
        ...     after=3, form='agent'
        ... )
        >>> type(agents.check().prepare().run(SimpleNamespace()).laws_[0]).__name__
        'RuleLaw'
        >>> separation = TransitionInteraction(
        ...     'separation', target='workers', state='employer', source=lambda value: value >= 0, destination=-1,
        ...     probability=lambda a, m: 0.05, form='agent'
        ... )
        >>> separation.check().destination
        -1
    """

    def _check_kind(self) -> str:
        """Raise an error for a transition without exactly one callable kind, or with an emission it cannot take."""
        kinds = [kind for kind in KINDS if getattr(self, kind) is not None]
        if len(kinds) != 1 or not callable(getattr(self, kinds[0])):
            error_msg = (
                f'The transition {self.label!r} has the forms {kinds}. Give exactly one of {", ".join(KINDS)}, as a '
                'function of the namespace.'
            )
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        unpaired = (self.emission is None) != (self.observed is None)
        if unpaired or (self.emission is not None and self.matrix is None):
            error_msg = (
                f'The transition {self.label!r} has the emission {self.emission!r} of the state {self.observed!r}. '
                'Give both with a matrix, or neither.'
            )
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        return kinds[0]

    def _check_form(self) -> None:
        """Raise an error for a transition without exactly one callable kind, or with arguments its kind ignores."""
        kind = self._check_kind()
        if self.over not in OVERS:
            error_msg = f'The transition {self.label!r} runs over {self.over!r}. Use one of {", ".join(OVERS)}.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if self.form not in FORMS:
            error_msg = f'The form of transition {self.label!r} is {self.form!r}. Use one of {", ".join(FORMS)}.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if self.schedule not in SCHEDULES or (self.form == 'symbolic' and self.schedule != 'synchronous'):
            error_msg = (
                f'The schedule of transition {self.label!r} is {self.schedule!r}. Use one of {", ".join(SCHEDULES)}, '
                'and the agent form for another than `synchronous`.'
            )
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if self.over == 'cell' and (self.form != 'agent' or kind == 'matrix' or self.after is not None):
            error_msg = (
                f'The members of transition {self.label!r} act on their cell, which only the agent form does, by a '
                'rate, a probability, or a condition and without a residence time.'
            )
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if self.over == 'neighbors' and (kind not in {'rate', 'probability'} or self.form == 'agent'):
            error_msg = (
                f'The transition {self.label!r} runs over neighbours, which only a rate or a probability of the '
                'symbolic form does. In the agent form, read the neighbours of the agent in the function.'
            )
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        single = not (callable(self.source) or isinstance(self.source, Collection))
        if self.matrix is None and single and self.source == self.destination:
            error_msg = f'The transition {self.label!r} moves from {self.source} to itself. Give distinct values.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        if self.after is not None and (kind not in {'probability', 'condition'} or not self.after >= 0):
            error_msg = (
                f'The transition {self.label!r} has the residence time {self.after!r}. Give a number of steps at least '
                '0 with a probability or a condition.'
            )
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)

    def _check_transition(self) -> None:
        """Raise an error for arguments the transition cannot take."""
        self._check_form()

    def _check(self) -> None:
        """Raise an error for a transition without exactly one kind, or with arguments its kind ignores.

        Raises:
            ValidationError:
                The transition has not exactly one kind, the source and the destination are equal, an emission has
                no matrix or no observed state, `over` is unknown or goes with a condition, a matrix, or the agent
                form, the residence time is negative or goes with a rate or a matrix, the form is unknown, or a
                function is not callable.
        """
        super()._check()
        self._check_transition()

    def _get_moving(self, value: Expression, source: SourceValues) -> Expression:
        """Return 1 for a member whose state holds a source value, and 0 otherwise."""
        if callable(source):
            return where(SR(source(value)), 1, 0)
        if isinstance(source, Collection):
            return sum((where(value == item, 1, 0) for item in set(source)), SR(0))
        return where(value == source, 1, 0)

    def _get_jump(self, value: Expression, source: SourceValues, destination: float, rate: Expression) -> JumpLaw:
        """Return the jump of the state from a source value to the destination at a rate."""
        if callable(source) or isinstance(source, Collection):
            return JumpLaw(rate * self._get_moving(value, source), {value: destination - value})
        return JumpLaw(rate * self._get_moving(value, source), {value: destination - source})

    def _get_step(self, value: Expression, source: SourceValues, destination: float, moves: Expression) -> UpdateLaw:
        """Return the update of the state that moves a member from a source value where a condition holds."""
        if callable(source) or isinstance(source, Collection):
            return UpdateLaw(value, where(self._get_moving(value, source) > 0, where(moves, destination, value), value))
        return UpdateLaw(value, where(value == source, where(moves, destination, source), value), source=source)

    def _holds_source(self, value: float) -> bool:
        """Return whether the value of the state of an agent is a source value."""
        if callable(self.source):
            return bool(self.source(value))
        if isinstance(self.source, Collection):
            return value in set(self.source)
        return value == self.source

    def _combine_rates(self, rate: Expression) -> Expression:
        """Return the rate of the member, or the sum of the rates of its neighbours."""
        return neighbors(rate, over=self.neighborhood) if self.over == 'neighbors' else rate

    def _combine_probabilities(self, probability: Expression) -> Expression:
        """Return the probability of the member, or the probability that at least one neighbour moves it."""
        if self.over == 'member':
            return probability
        return 1 - exp(neighbors(log(1 - probability), over=self.neighborhood))

    def _get_draw(self) -> Expression:
        """Return the uniform draw of the transition in a step."""
        return uniform(re.sub(r'\W', '_', f'transition_{self.label}'))

    def _get_chain(self, namespace: SimpleNamespace, value: Expression) -> list[BasePart]:
        """Return the transition law of the state by its matrix, with the emission law of the observed state."""
        laws: list[BasePart] = []
        if self.matrix is not None:
            laws.append(TransitionLaw(value, self.matrix(namespace), label=self.label))
        if self.emission is not None and self.observed is not None:
            observed = getattr(self._get_member(namespace, 'target'), self.observed)
            laws.append(EmissionLaw(observed, value, self.emission(namespace), label=self.label))
        return laws

    def _get_held(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> int:
        """Return the number of whole steps an agent has held its value, counted in the storage of the model."""
        value = float(getattr(agent, self.state))
        record = model.storage.get(f'{self.label}.{agent.id}')
        held = record[1] + 1 if isinstance(record, tuple) and record[0] == value else 0
        model.storage[f'{self.label}.{agent.id}'] = (value, held)
        return held

    def _draw_row(self, row: Sequence[object], model: ModelViewProtocol) -> float:
        """Return a value drawn by the probabilities of a row of a matrix."""
        weights = np.asarray(row, dtype=float)
        return float(model.rng.choice(weights.size, p=weights / weights.sum()))

    def _evaluate[T](
        self,
        function: Callable[..., T],
        agent: AgentViewProtocol,
        other: AgentViewProtocol | None,
        model: ModelViewProtocol,
    ) -> T:
        """Return a function of an agent, or of an agent and the member of its cell that acts on it."""
        return function(agent, model) if other is None else function(agent, other, model)

    def _moves_agent(
        self,
        agent: AgentViewProtocol,
        model: ModelViewProtocol,
        other: AgentViewProtocol | None = None,
    ) -> bool:
        """Return whether an agent that holds the source value moves in this step, moved by another one or alone."""
        if self.condition is not None:
            return bool(self._evaluate(self.condition, agent, other, model))
        if self.rate is not None:
            probability = 1.0 - math.exp(-float(self._evaluate(self.rate, agent, other, model)))
        elif self.probability is not None:
            probability = float(self._evaluate(self.probability, agent, other, model))
        else:
            probability = 0.0
        return bool(model.rng.random() < probability)

    def _get_next(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> float | None:
        """Return the next value of the state of an agent, or `None` when it keeps its value."""
        value = float(getattr(agent, self.state))
        if self.matrix is not None:
            return self._draw_row(self.matrix(agent, model)[int(value)], model)
        held = self._get_held(agent, model) if self.after is not None else 0
        if not self._holds_source(value) or (self.after is not None and held + 1 < self.after):
            return None
        return float(self.destination) if self._moves_agent(agent, model) else None

    def _get_moves(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> list[tuple[AgentViewProtocol, float]]:
        """Return the members an agent moves in this step, itself or the members of its cell, with their values."""
        if self.over != 'cell':
            value = self._get_next(agent, model)
            return [] if value is None else [(agent, value)]
        return [
            (other, float(self.destination))
            for other in agent.agents_here(agent.group)
            if other is not agent
            and self._holds_source(float(getattr(other, self.state)))
            and self._moves_agent(other, model, agent)
        ]

    def _apply(self, moves: list[tuple[AgentViewProtocol, float]], model: ModelViewProtocol) -> None:
        """Set the moved states, and draw the observed state of an emission."""
        for agent, value in moves:
            setattr(agent, self.state, value)
            if self.emission is not None and self.observed is not None:
                setattr(agent, self.observed, self._draw_row(self.emission(agent, model)[int(value)], model))

    def _move_member(self, agent: AgentViewProtocol, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> None:
        """Move the state of one member, or of the members of its cell, from the states the members before it set."""
        if agent.alive:
            self._apply(self._get_moves(agent, model), model)

    def move(self, model: ModelViewProtocol) -> None:
        """Move the state of every agent of the target that moves in this step, by the schedule.

        Args:
            model:
                The model view of the interaction.
        """
        agents = list(model.agents(self._get_role('target')))
        if self.schedule == 'synchronous':
            moves = [move for agent in agents for move in self._get_moves(agent, model)]
            self._apply(moves, model)
            return
        order = model.rng.permutation(len(agents)) if self.schedule == 'random-sequential' else range(len(agents))
        for index in order:
            self._move_member(agents[int(index)], model)

    def _get_laws(self, namespace: SimpleNamespace) -> list[BasePart]:
        """Return the jump, the update, the transition or emission law of the state, or the rule of the agent form.

        Args:
            namespace:
                The namespace of the symbols the laws can use, with `namespace.target`.

        Returns:
            laws:
                The law of the move, or the rule that moves the agents.
        """
        if self.form == 'agent' and self.schedule == 'member':
            return [RuleLaw(self._move_member, phase=self.phase, members=self._get_role('target'))]
        if self.form == 'agent':
            return [RuleLaw(self.move, phase=self.phase)]
        value = getattr(self._get_member(namespace, 'target'), self.state)
        if self.matrix is not None:
            return self._get_chain(namespace, value)
        if self.rate is not None:
            rate = self._combine_rates(SR(self.rate(namespace)))
            return [self._get_jump(value, self.source, self.destination, rate)]
        if self.probability is not None:
            probability = self._combine_probabilities(SR(self.probability(namespace)))
            moves = self._get_draw() < probability
        else:
            moves = SR(self.condition(namespace)) if self.condition is not None else SR(0)
        if self.after is not None:
            moves = both(residence(value) + 1 >= self.after, moves)
        return [self._get_step(value, self.source, self.destination, moves)]

    replicator_dynamics

    The replicator dynamics of a game, by default hawks and doves, which settle at a share V / C of hawks.

    • Field: biology/evolution
    • Formalism: ordinary differential equations
    • References: Taylor and Jonker 1978; Maynard Smith 1982; Gros 2015; Szabó and Fáth 2007

    Example

    Factory

    @register_model(
    field='biology/evolution',
    tags=('evolutionary game', 'replicator', 'ode', 'game theory'),
    references=('Taylor and Jonker 1978', 'Maynard Smith 1982', 'Gros 2015', 'Szabó and Fáth 2007'),
    defaults={'until': 30.0, 'dt': 0.1},
)
def replicator_dynamics(
    x: Sequence[float] | None = None,
    *,
    payoff: Sequence[Sequence[float]] | None = None,
    V: float = 2.0,
    C: float = 3.0,
) -> System:
    """Return the replicator dynamics of a game, by default hawks and doves, which settle at a share `V / C` of hawks.

    Every strategy is a member of the group `population`, and it grows with its payoff above the mean payoff. The
    hawks and doves game has the payoff matrix `((V - C) / 2, V), (0, V / 2)` with hawks first. When the cost of a fight
    `C` exceeds the value `V`, hawks and doves coexist with `V / C` hawks, and otherwise hawks take over. A payoff
    matrix gives any other game, such as the prisoner's dilemma or rock, paper, scissors.

    Args:
        x:
            The initial shares of the strategies, or `None` for equal shares.

        payoff:
            The payoff matrix of another game, one row per strategy, or `None` for hawks and doves.

        V:
            The value of the resource contested by hawks and doves.

        C:
            The cost of a fight between hawks.

    Returns:
        system:
            The model, with the shares `population.x` of the strategies, hawks first.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.biology.evolution import replicator_dynamics
        >>> replicator_dynamics(payoff=((0, -1, 1), (1, 0, -1), (-1, 1, 0))).elements[0].check().prepare().size_
        3
    """
    matrix = (((V - C) / 2, V), (0.0, V / 2)) if payoff is None else tuple(tuple(row) for row in payoff)
    strategies = len(matrix)
    shares = [1 / strategies] * strategies if x is None else list(x)
    population = GroupElement('population', element=StrategyElement, size=strategies, values={'x': shares})
    weights = np.asarray(matrix, dtype=float)
    replication = ReplicatorInteraction(
        'replication',
        target='population',
        fitness=lambda s: total(s.target.x, weights=weights),
    )
    return System(elements=[population], interactions=[replication])

class ReplicatorInteraction(RateInteraction):
    """Replicator equation of the shares of a group, `dx_i/dt = x_i (f_i - sum_j x_j f_j)`, with a fitness `f`.

    Every member of the group holds the share of a type, such as a strategy or a molecule. Its share grows with its
    fitness above the mean fitness of the group, so the total of the shares stays constant.

    Args:
        label:
            The label of the interaction.

        target:
            The path of the group of types.

        fitness:
            The function of the namespace that returns the fitness of a member, such as
            `lambda s: total(s.target.x, weights=payoff)` for the payoff of a strategy against the others.

        state:
            The name of the state of the members that holds the share.

    Raises:
        ValidationError:
            The fitness is not callable, raised by `check`.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.core.elements import GroupElement
        >>> from skcomplex.core.laws.utils import total
        >>> from skcomplex.core.system import System
        >>> from skcomplex.engine.runtime import Simulation
        >>> from skcomplex.models.biology.evolution import ReplicatorInteraction, StrategyElement
        >>> population = GroupElement('population', element=StrategyElement, size=2, values={'x': [0.5, 0.5]})
        >>> payoff = ((1.0, 1.0), (0.0, 0.0))
        >>> replication = ReplicatorInteraction(
        ...     'replication', target='population', fitness=lambda s: total(s.target.x, weights=payoff)
        ... )
        >>> system = System(elements=[population], interactions=[replication])
        >>> trajectory = Simulation(system, dt=0.1).check().prepare().run(until=20.0).trajectory_
        >>> [round(float(share), 3) for share in trajectory.values('population.x')[-1]]
        [1.0, 0.0]
    """

    def __init__(
        self,
        label: str,
        *,
        target: Annotated[str, RoleSpec()],
        fitness: Callable[[SimpleNamespace], object] | None = None,
        state: str = 'x',
    ) -> None:
        super().__init__(label, target=target, state=state)
        self.fitness = fitness

    def _check_fitness(self) -> None:
        """Raise an error for a fitness that is not callable, the hook a subclass that derives its fitness overrides."""
        if not callable(self.fitness):
            error_msg = f'The fitness of {self.label!r} is {self.fitness!r}. Give a function of the namespace.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)

    def _check(self) -> None:
        """Raise an error for a state, functions, or a fitness the interaction cannot take.

        Raises:
            ValidationError:
                The state is not a name, or the fitness is not callable.
        """
        super()._check()
        self._check_fitness()

    def _get_fitness(self, namespace: SimpleNamespace) -> Expression:
        """Return the fitness of a member, the hook a subclass overrides to derive it from its parameters."""
        fitness = self.fitness
        if fitness is None:
            error_msg = f'The fitness of {self.label!r} is missing. Give a function of the namespace.'
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
        value: Expression = fitness(namespace)
        return value

    def _get_rate(self, namespace: SimpleNamespace) -> Expression:
        """Return the replicator rate `x_i (f_i - sum_j x_j f_j)`.

        Args:
            namespace:
                The namespace of the symbols the rate can use, with the namespace of the target.

        Returns:
            rate:
                The rate of the share.
        """
        share = getattr(namespace.target, self.state)
        fitness = self._get_fitness(namespace)
        rate: Expression = share * (fitness - total(share * fitness))
        return rate

    field='biology/systems_biology',
    tags=('boolean network', 'repressilator', 'oscillator', 'discrete map', 'delay'),
    references=(RAMAN, 'Elowitz and Leibler 2000'),
    defaults={'until': 24, 'dt': 1},
)
def boolean_repressilator(x: Sequence[int] = (1, 0, 0), *, delay: int = 0) -> System:
    """Return the Boolean repressilator, where LacI represses TetR, TetR represses cI, and cI represses LacI.

    The transfer functions are `TetR* = NOT LacI`, `cI* = NOT TetR`, and `LacI* = NOT cI`. From a state with one or two
    genes on, the genes switch on and off in turn with period 6, and the states `000` and `111` alternate with period
    2. A repression delayed by `delay` steps passes through that many relay nodes, `<repressor>_1` and so on, which
    copy the repressor one step later and start at its initial value. The oscillation remains, with period
    `6 (delay + 1)`.

    Args:
        x:
            The initial values of LacI, TetR, and cI.

        delay:
            The number of steps by which every repression is delayed.

    Returns:
        system:
            The model, with the values `LacI.x`, `TetR.x`, `cI.x`, and the relays `<repressor>_<k>.x`.

    Raises:
        ValidationError:
            The delay is negative.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.biology.systems_biology import boolean_repressilator
        >>> [node.label for node in boolean_repressilator(delay=1).elements]
        ['LacI', 'TetR', 'cI', 'LacI_1', 'TetR_1', 'cI_1']
    """
    if delay < 0:
        error_msg = f'The delay must be a nonnegative number of steps, not {delay}.'
        raise ValidationError(error_msg)
    genes = [BooleanNodeElement(name, x=int(value)) for name, value in zip(REPRESSILATOR_GENES, x, strict=True)]
    relays = [BooleanNodeElement(f'{gene.label}_{step}', x=gene.x) for step in range(1, delay + 1) for gene in genes]
    functions: list[BooleanFunctionInteraction] = []
    for repressor, target in zip(REPRESSILATOR_GENES, (*REPRESSILATOR_GENES[1:], REPRESSILATOR_GENES[0]), strict=True):
        chain = [repressor, *(f'{repressor}_{step}' for step in range(1, delay + 1))]
        functions.extend(
            BooleanFunctionInteraction(f'relay_{relay}', target=relay, inputs=[source], table=(0, 1))
            for source, relay in itertools.pairwise(chain)
        )
        functions.append(
            BooleanFunctionInteraction(f'repression_{target}', target=target, inputs=[chain[-1]], table=(1, 0)),
        )
    return System(elements=[*genes, *relays], interactions=functions)

class BooleanFunctionInteraction(MapInteraction):
    """Synchronous update of a node by a truth table of any number of input nodes.

    A table of `2^k` entries reads the `k` inputs as the bits of a binary number, from the most significant, and gives
    the next value of the target. The update is the sum, over the patterns whose entry is 1, of the product that is 1
    exactly on that pattern.

    Args:
        label:
            The label of the interaction.

        target:
            The path of the updated node.

        inputs:
            The paths of the input nodes, in the order of the bits of the table.

        table:
            The next value of the target for each of the `2^k` input patterns.

    Raises:
        ValidationError:
            The table does not have one entry per pattern of the inputs.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.biology.systems_biology import BooleanFunctionInteraction
        >>> BooleanFunctionInteraction('and', target='c', inputs=['a', 'b'], table=(0, 0, 0, 1)).table
        (0, 0, 0, 1)
    """

    def __init__(
        self,
        label: str,
        *,
        target: Annotated[str, RoleSpec()],
        inputs: Annotated[list[str], RoleSpec(variadic=True)],
        table: tuple[int, ...] = (0,),
    ) -> None:
        super().__init__(label, target=target, state='x', function=self._get_next)
        self.inputs = inputs
        self.table = table

    def _get_pattern(self, inputs: Sequence[Expression], index: int) -> Expression:
        """Return the product that is 1 when the inputs, read as bits from the most significant, spell an index."""
        bits = [(index >> (len(inputs) - 1 - position)) & 1 for position in range(len(inputs))]
        return prod((value if bit else 1 - value for value, bit in zip(inputs, bits, strict=True)), start=SR(1))

    def _get_next(self, s: SimpleNamespace) -> Expression:
        """Return the next value of the target by the truth table."""
        inputs = [item.x for item in s.inputs]
        terms = [self._get_pattern(inputs, index) for index, entry in enumerate(self.table) if entry]
        return sum(terms, start=SR(0))

    def _check(self) -> None:
        """Raise an error for a table without one entry per pattern of the inputs.

        Raises:
            ValidationError:
                The table does not have one entry per pattern of the inputs.
        """
        super()._check()
        if len(self.table) != 2 ** len(self.inputs):
            error_msg = (
                f'BooleanFunction {self.label} reads {len(self.inputs)} inputs, which take {2 ** len(self.inputs)} '
                f'patterns, but its table has {len(self.table)} entries.'
            )
            raise ValidationError(error_msg)
