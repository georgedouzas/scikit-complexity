    CarlemanGasElement(label, visual=None, *, f_plus=1.0, f_minus=1.0, c=1.0, kappa=1.0)

    Bases: Element

    Site of the Carleman discrete-velocity gas, densities f_plus and f_minus moving at the speeds c and -c.

    Collisions turn two particles of one velocity into two of the other at the rate kappa, so df_plus/dt + c df_plus/dx = kappa (f_minus^2 - f_plus^2) and the opposite for f_minus, which conserves the mass and relaxes to the equilibrium f_plus = f_minus.

    FHPSiteElement(label, visual=None, *, n0=0, n1=0, n2=0, n3=0, n4=0, n5=0, density=0)

    Bases: Element

    Site of the FHP-I lattice gas, with one boolean channel per direction of the triangular lattice.

    The local rules of the gas collide the particles at every site, head-on pairs turning by a sixth of a turn to either side with equal probability and symmetric triples turning by a sixth of a turn, and then move every particle to the neighbouring site along its direction. Collisions conserve the number of particles and their momentum at every site. The state density holds the number of particles at the site.

    benard_marangoni(shape=(64, 64), *, length=16 * math.pi, r=0.1, g=1.0, random_state=0)

    Return the order-parameter equation of Bénard-Marangoni convection, which forms hexagonal cells.

    The field u is the vertical velocity at mid-depth of a heated layer whose open surface has a surface tension that falls with temperature. It follows u_t = r u - (1 + laplacian)^2 u + g u^2 - u^3, the Swift-Hohenberg equation of convection with the quadratic term that the thermocapillary surface adds by breaking the symmetry between rising and sinking fluid. With g > 0 and small r > 0, the cells are hexagons of rising fluid with a wavenumber near 1, and with g = 0, the layer of a fixed surface, they are rolls.

    spacing = 2 * math.pi / n
    centers = (np.arange(n) + 0.5) * spacing
    values = {'k': k0 * (1 + amplitude * np.sin(centers)), 'g': g}
    space = LatticeSpace(n, spacing=spacing)
    return System(
        elements=[
            FieldElement(
                'train',
                element=WaveNumberElement,
                space=space,
                values=values,
                discretization='finite-volume',
            ),
        ],
        interactions=[
            ConservationInteraction('action', target='train', state='k', flux=lambda s: sqrt(s.target.g * s.target.k)),
        ],
    )
