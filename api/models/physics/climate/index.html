    Bases: Element

    Ocean of Rothman's excitable carbon cycle, its carbonate ion concentration and dissolved inorganic carbon.

    The carbonate ion concentration c and the dissolved inorganic carbon w, in μmol kg⁻¹, follow dc/dt = f(c) (mu (1 - b s(c, c_p) - theta (1 - s(c, c_x)) - nu) + w - w0) and dw/dt = mu (1 - b s(c, c_p) + theta (1 - s(c, c_x)) + nu) - w + w0, with the Hill functions s(c, c*) = c^gamma / (c^gamma + c*^gamma) of burial and respiration and the buffer f(c) = f0 c^beta / (c^beta + c_f^beta).

    EarlyWarningAnalysis(variables=None, *, window=None, detrend=True)

    Bases: RollingAnalysis

    Rolling indicators of critical slowing down before a tipping point, for every series of a source.

    It is the rolling analysis of kind 'autocorrelation' with detrended windows, whose statistic and trend are the lag-one autocorrelation, and it adds the other indicators of Dakos et al. (2012): the rolling variance and skewness, and the reddening, the ratio of the mean power of the lowest eighth of frequencies to that of the highest half, which rises as fluctuations slow down. The trend of every indicator is Kendall's tau against time.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    variables Sequence[str] | None

    The variables of a trajectory to read, or None for every scalar series.

    		 None
    window int | None

    The number of values in every rolling window.

    		 None
    detrend bool

    Whether to remove a linear trend in every window first.

    		 True

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    variance_

    The variance of every series in every window, nan before the first full window.
    autocorrelation_

    The lag-1 autocorrelation of every series in every window, nan before the first full window.
    skewness_

    The skewness of every series in every window, nan before the first full window.
    reddening_

    The reddening of every series in every window, nan before the first full window.
    variance_trend_

    Kendall's tau of the variance of every series against time, positive when it rises.
    autocorrelation_trend_

    Kendall's tau of the autocorrelation of every series against time, positive when it rises.
    skewness_trend_

    Kendall's tau of the skewness of every series against time.
    reddening_trend_

    Kendall's tau of the reddening of every series against time.

    Bases: Element

    Energy sector of a society, a finite reserve, the capacity that extracts it, and the population it supplies.

    The capacity draws the gross energy capacity reserve / reserve_size from the reserve. Part of it pays for the extraction work, the gross energy divided by the energy return on investment 1 + (initial_eroi - 1) (reserve / reserve_size)^decline, and the rest is the net energy society receives. A share reinvestment of the net energy builds capacity, which depreciates over lifetime, and the population grows at the rate birth_rate - death_rate.

        'surface',
        target='soil',
        state='x',
        function=lambda s: s.target.r * s.target.x * (1 - s.target.x) + s.target.epsilon * s.target.y,
