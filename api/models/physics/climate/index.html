    Climate

    Climate: energy balance, variability, glacial cycles, low-order atmospheres, net energy, tipping and its warnings.

    CarbonCycleElement(label, visual=None, *, c=83.58, w=2050.0, mu=250.0, b=4.0, theta=5.0, c_x=55.0, c_p=110.0, nu=0.0, w0=2000.0, gamma=4.0, f0=0.694, c_f=43.9, beta=1.7)

    Bases: Element

    Ocean of Rothman's excitable carbon cycle, its carbonate ion concentration and dissolved inorganic carbon.

    The carbonate ion concentration c and the dissolved inorganic carbon w, in μmol kg⁻¹, follow dc/dt = f(c) (mu (1 - b s(c, c_p) - theta (1 - s(c, c_x)) - nu) + w - w0) and dw/dt = mu (1 - b s(c, c_p) + theta (1 - s(c, c_x)) + nu) - w + w0, with the Hill functions s(c, c*) = c^gamma / (c^gamma + c*^gamma) of burial and respiration and the buffer f(c) = f0 c^beta / (c^beta + c_f^beta).

    Name Type Description Default
    label str

    The label of the ocean.

    		 required
    visual VisualSpec | None

    How the element is drawn, or None for default_visual_.

    		 None
    c Annotated[float, StateSpec(domain=open(0, oo))]

    The initial carbonate ion concentration.

    		 83.58
    w Annotated[float, StateSpec(domain=open(0, oo))]

    The initial dissolved inorganic carbon.

    		 2050.0
    mu Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The characteristic rate of the carbon fluxes.

    		 250.0
    b Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The maximal burial of organic carbon.

    		 4.0
    theta Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The maximal respiration of organic carbon.

    		 5.0
    c_x Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The crossover carbonate concentration of respiration, which sets the excitability.

    		 55.0
    c_p Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The crossover carbonate concentration of burial.

    		 110.0

    The rate of external carbon injection.

    		 0.0
    w0 Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The input of dissolved inorganic carbon by weathering and volcanism.

    		 2000.0
    gamma Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The Hill coefficient of burial and respiration.

    		 4.0
    f0 Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The maximal buffer of the carbonate ion concentration.

    		 0.694
    c_f Annotated[float, ParameterSpec()]

    The carbonate concentration where the buffer is half its maximum.

    		 43.9

    and the deeper soil temperature `y` relaxes towards it: `x_{n+1} = r x_n (1 - x_n) + epsilon y_n` and

            The logistic parameter of the surface energy balance.

            The coupling between the surface and the deeper soil.

        system:
            The model, with the temperatures `soil.x` and `soil.y`.

        >>> from skcomplex.models.physics.climate import soil_temperature
        >>> soil_temperature(r=2.5).elements[0].r

        'surface',
        target='soil',
        state='x',
        function=lambda s: s.target.r * s.target.x * (1 - s.target.x) + s.target.epsilon * s.target.y,
    )

        'depth',
        target='soil',
        state='y',
        function=lambda s: s.target.epsilon * (s.target.x + s.target.y),
    )
    return System(
        elements=[SoilTemperatureElement('soil', x=x, y=y, r=r, epsilon=epsilon)],
