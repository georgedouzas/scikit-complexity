    Criticality

    Criticality: sandpiles, forest fires, absorbing-state transitions, learners at the edge, and avalanches.

    ActiveFieldElement(label, visual=None, *, rho=0.5, nu=1.0, a=-1.0, b=1.0, c=1.0, d=1.0, e=0.2, f=0.0, g=0.0)

    Bases: Element

    Site of the Langevin equation of directed percolation with a conserved field, an activity rho and a field nu.

    The activity grows at the rate a + b nu, saturates, diffuses, and fluctuates with the multiplicative noise e sqrt(rho) of an absorbing state, rho_t = (a + b nu) rho - c rho^2 + d laplacian(rho) + e sqrt(rho) eta. The field diffuses, gains f, and is consumed by the activity, nu_t = laplacian(nu) + f - g rho. Both stay nonnegative.

    AvalancheInteraction(label, *, target, state='height', threshold=4, mode='redistribute')

    Bases: CascadeInteraction

    Slowly driven sandpile: every step drops one grain on a random site, and the full sites topple in avalanches.

    A site that holds threshold grains topples. In the mode 'redistribute' it sends one grain to each of its neighbours, as in the Bak-Tang-Wiesenfeld sandpile, and in the mode 'random' every grain goes to a neighbour drawn at random, as in the Manna sandpile. Grains that topple over a fixed edge leave the pile, and every avalanche is recorded as an event under the label of the interaction.

    The label of the interaction and of its avalanche events.

    The path of the lattice group of sites.

    		 required
    state str

    The name of the state that holds the grains.

    		 'height'
    threshold int

    The number of grains at which a site topples.

    		 4
    mode str

    'redistribute' or 'random'.

    		 'redistribute'

    Examples:

    CracklingNoiseAnalysis(variables=None, *, threshold=0.0, durations=(), min_count=10, min_duration=1, size_exponent=None, duration_exponent=None)

    Bases: EventAnalysis

    Crackling-noise scaling of the avalanches of an activity series or of the relaxations of a lattice.

    It finds the avalanches as the event analysis of kind 'excursions', then fits the exponent gamma of the mean size against the duration, <S>(T) ~ T^gamma, predicts it as (tau_T - 1) / (tau_S - 1) from the exponents of the size and duration laws, such as those FitAnalysis(law='power') fits, and collapses the mean shapes of the chosen durations, which agree after scaling at a critical point.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    variables Sequence[str] | None

    The one variable of a trajectory to read, or None for the only scalar series.

    		 None
    threshold float

    The activity an avalanche exceeds at every step, subtracted from its profile.

    		 0.0
    durations Sequence[int]

    The durations whose mean shapes are compared.

    		 ()
    min_count int

    The fewest avalanches of a duration used in the fit of the mean size.

    		 10
    min_duration int

    The shortest duration used in the fit of the mean size.

    		 1
    size_exponent float | None

    The exponent tau_S of the power law of the sizes, or None to leave out the prediction.

    		 None
    duration_exponent float | None

    The exponent tau_T of the power law of the durations, or None to leave out the prediction.

    		 None

    Attributes:

    Name Type Description
    exponent_

    The exponent gamma of the mean size against the duration.
    predicted_

    mean_shapes_

    shape_spread_

    The mean over the inner 80 percent of the scaled time of the relative difference between the largest and the smallest mean shape of the chosen durations, nan with fewer than 2 of them.

    LearnerElement(label, visual=None, *, incentive=0.0, spin=1.0, temperature=1.0, fitness=0.0, reward=0.0, memory=0.75, max_temperature=2.0, couplings=None, evolving=True, settling=2)

    Bases: Element

    Agent of the evolutionary Sherrington-Kirkpatrick game, who learns a binary action and may be replaced.

    The agent keeps an incentive Q, the value of action +1, and plays the action s = ±1 by the softmax policy at its temperature T. Every step the incentive becomes (1 - alpha) Q + alpha h for the local field h = Σ_j J_ij s_j, and the fitness is s Q. When the agent evolves and its fitness is the least of the group, it is replaced by a newcomer with no incentive and a temperature drawn uniformly up to max_temperature, who learns from the field at once. The reward s h of the last actions is kept.

    directed_percolation_langevin(shape=(64, 64), *, a=-1.0, b=1.0, c=1.0, d=1.0, e=0.2, f=0.0, g=0.0, energy=1.2, density=0.5, random_state=0)

    Return the Langevin field theory of an absorbing-state transition, an active density fed by an energy field.

    The active density rho grows at the local rate a + b nu, saturates through -c rho^2, spreads with the diffusivity d, and fluctuates with the multiplicative noise e sqrt(rho), which vanishes in the absorbing state rho = 0. The energy field nu diffuses, gains the drive f, and loses the dissipation g rho, so rho_t = (a + b nu) rho - c rho^2 + d laplacian(rho) + e sqrt(rho) xi and nu_t = laplacian(nu) + f - g rho (Tian et al., Eqs. 3-4). Both fields stay nonnegative. Without drive and dissipation the energy is a fixed control parameter: below the threshold nu_c = -a / b activity dies out, and above it the density settles near (a + b nu) / c.

    a float

    		 -1.0
    b float

    The gain of the growth rate per unit of energy.

    		 1.0
    c float

    The saturation of the activity.

    		 1.0
    d float

    		 1.0

    b: float = 1.0,
    c: float = 1.0,
    d: float = 1.0,
    e: float = 0.2,
    f: float = 0.0,

    """Return the Langevin field theory of an absorbing-state transition, an active density fed by an energy field.

    The active density `rho` grows at the local rate `a + b nu`, saturates through `-c rho^2`, spreads with the
    diffusivity `d`, and fluctuates with the multiplicative noise `e sqrt(rho)`, which vanishes in the absorbing state
    `rho = 0`. The energy field `nu` diffuses, gains the drive `f`, and loses the dissipation `g rho`, so
    `rho_t = (a + b nu) rho - c rho^2 + d laplacian(rho) + e sqrt(rho) xi` and `nu_t = laplacian(nu) + f - g rho`

            The number of sites along every axis of the periodic lattice.

        a:
            The bare growth rate of the activity.

        b:
            The gain of the growth rate per unit of energy.

        c:
            The saturation of the activity.

        d:
            The diffusivity of the activity.

        e:
            The amplitude of the multiplicative noise.

        f:
            The drive of the energy.

        g:
            The dissipation of the energy per unit of activity.

        energy:
            The initial energy at every site.

        density:
            The mean initial activity, drawn uniformly between 0 and twice this value.

        random_state:
            The seed or generator of the initial activity.

    Returns:
        system:
            The model, with the activity `neurons.rho` and the energy `neurons.nu`.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.physics.criticality import directed_percolation_langevin
        >>> float(directed_percolation_langevin((8, 8), energy=0.5).elements[0].values['nu'].mean())
        0.5
    """
    shape = tuple(shape)
    rng = np.random.default_rng(random_state)
    values = {
        'rho': rng.uniform(0.0, 2 * density, shape),
        'nu': np.full(shape, energy),
        'a': a,
        'b': b,
        'c': c,
        'd': d,
        'e': e,
        'f': f,
        'g': g,
    }
    return System(
        elements=[FieldElement('neurons', element=ActiveFieldElement, space=LatticeSpace(shape), values=values)],

    upper = np.triu(rng.normal(0.0, 1.0 / np.sqrt(n_agents), (n_agents, n_agents)), 1)
    agents = GroupElement(
        'agents',
        element=LearningAgentElement,
        size=n_agents,
        values={
            'sigma': rng.choice([-1.0, 1.0], size=n_agents),
            'Q': 0.0,
            'alpha': alpha,
            'temperature': temperature,
            'couplings': upper + upper.T,
        },
    )
    return System(elements=[agents])
