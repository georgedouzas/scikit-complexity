    ActiveHostElement(label, visual=None, *, i=0.0, a=0.1, lam=0.3, mu=0.1)

    A TransitionInteraction recovers an infected node with probability mu in a step, and another infects a susceptible node with probability lam by every infected neighbour along the edges of the previous step.     CarrierElement(label, visual=None, *, p=0.05, lam=0.2)

    Bases: Element

    Node of a network whose state is the probability that it is infected, in quenched mean field.

    The node recovers at rate 1 and every infected neighbour infects it at rate lam, with the probabilities of the neighbours taken as independent, so dp_i / dt = -p_i + lam (1 - p_i) sum_j A_ij p_j over the neighbours j.

    Bases: Element

    Infected density of the nodes of one degree of a network, in heterogeneous mean field.

    A node of degree k recovers at rate 1 and is infected at rate lam k Theta, where Theta = sum_k k P(k) rho_k / <k> is the probability that an edge points to an infected node, a total over the degree classes, so d rho_k / dt = -rho_k + lam k (1 - rho_k) Theta. The classes are the members of a group, and the recovery and the infection are interactions of the group, which heterogeneous_mean_field_sis adds.

    ImmunizingDegreeClassElement(label, visual=None, *, s=0.999, i=0.001, r=0.0, beta=0.1, mu=1.0, k=1.0, P=1.0)

    Bases: Element

    Susceptible, infected, and recovered densities of the nodes of one degree, in heterogeneous mean field.

    A susceptible node of degree k is infected at rate beta k theta and an infected node recovers at rate mu, so d i_k / dt = -mu i_k + beta k s_k theta and d r_k / dt = mu i_k. On an uncorrelated network, the probability that a contact of a susceptible node is infected is theta = sum_k (k - 1) P(k) i_k / <k>, a total over the degree classes, since an infected neighbour of degree k has at most k - 1 other contacts. The classes are the members of a group, and the infections and the recoveries are interactions of the group, which heterogeneous_mean_field_sir adds.

    PersonElement(label, visual=VisualSpec('agents', {'color_by': 'health', 'color_range': (0.0, 4.0)}), *, health=SUSCEPTIBLE, d=0.8, beta=0.6, gamma=0.01, mu=0.0, alpha=0.0, lockdown_start=0.0, lockdown_length=0.0, lockdown_d=0.1)

    Bases: Element

    Person of the agent-based SIR model, with incubation, mortality, waning immunity, and lockdown.

    A MovementInteraction walks a living person to a neighbouring cell with probability d, or lockdown_d during the lockdown, which lasts lockdown_length steps from lockdown_start. Transitions of the agent form, which agent_based_sir adds, move the health of the person. An infectious person infects, with probability beta, every susceptible person in its cell, who becomes exposed. An infectious person recovers with probability gamma or dies with probability mu, and a recovered person loses immunity with probability alpha. Health is 0 susceptible, 1 exposed, 2 infected, 3 recovered, or 4 dead.

    The incubation follows the convention of the residence time after of TransitionInteraction: the step of the exposure counts as the first step in the exposed state, so a person exposed in step n becomes infectious at the end of step n + incubation - 1. The clock that the person kept before counted the step of the exposure only when the person acted after its infector in that step, so the onset now comes half a step earlier on average.

    @register_model(
    field='biology/epidemiology',
    tags=('temporal network', 'activity-driven', 'stochastic map', 'epidemic threshold'),
    references=('Perra et al. 2012', 'Pastor-Satorras et al. 2015'),
    defaults={'until': 150, 'dt': 1.0},
)
def activity_driven_epidemics(
    n: int = 500,
    *,
    m: int = 2,
    exponent: float = 2.2,
    least: float = 0.01,
    lam: float = 0.9,
    mu: float = 0.1,
    infected: float = 0.05,
    seed: int | None = 1,
) -> System:
    """Return SIS spreading on an activity-driven temporal network, whose edges last one step.

    At every step every node fires with probability its activity, drawn from `a^-exponent` above `least`, and joins
    `m` random nodes. The infection travels along the edges of a step, which disappear at the next step, so it spreads
    only when `lam / mu` exceeds a threshold set by the moments of the activity, `2 <a> / (m (<a> + sqrt(<a^2>)))` in
    the limit of small probabilities, and not by the degrees of the network aggregated over time.

    Args:
        n:
            The number of nodes.

        m:
            The number of edges an active node creates.

        exponent:
            The exponent of the power-law distribution of activities.

        least:
            The smallest activity.

        lam:
            The probability of infection along one edge.

        mu:
            The probability of recovery at a step.

        infected:
            The initial share of infected nodes.

        seed:
            The seed of the activities and the initial infections.

    Returns:
        system:
            The model, with the states `hosts.i` and activities `hosts.a` of the group `hosts` on the nodes, and the
            variable group `contacts` on the edges of every step.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.biology.epidemiology import activity_driven_epidemics
        >>> float(activity_driven_epidemics(n=10, seed=0).elements[0].values['a'].min()) >= 0.01
        True
    """
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    activities = np.minimum(least * (1 - rng.random(n)) ** (-1 / (exponent - 1)), 1.0)
    initial = (rng.random(n) < infected).astype(float)
    space = NetworkSpace(nx.empty_graph(n))
    hosts = GroupElement(
        'hosts',
        element=ActiveHostElement,
        space=space,
        on='sites',
        variable=True,
        capacity=n,
        values={'i': initial, 'a': activities, 'lam': lam, 'mu': mu},
        visual=VisualSpec('sites', {'states': ['i'], 'low': 0, 'high': 1}),
    )
    contacts = GroupElement(
        'contacts',
        element=Element,
        space=space,
        on='edges',
        variable=True,
        capacity=2 * m * n,
        visual=HIDDEN,
    )
    return System(
        elements=[hosts, contacts],
        interactions=[
            TopologyInteraction('firing', target='hosts', action='add-edge', probability=_get_activity, edges=m),
            TopologyInteraction('expiry', target='hosts', action='remove-edge'),
            TransitionInteraction(
                'infection',
                target='hosts',
                state='i',
                source=0,
                destination=1,
                probability=_get_infection,
            ),
            TransitionInteraction(
                'recovery',
                target='hosts',
                state='i',
                source=1,
                destination=0,
                probability=_get_recovery,
            ),
        ],
        time=DiscreteTime(),
    )

def sis(

        ['S', 'I']

            RecoveryInteraction('recovery', infected='I', recovered='S', gamma=gamma, stochastic=stochastic),
        ],
