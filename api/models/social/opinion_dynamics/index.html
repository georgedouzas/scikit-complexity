    AdoptionInteraction(label, *, target, mode='infection')

    Bases: TransitionInteraction

    In the infection mode an agent adopts once anyone in its social circle has adopted. In the influence mode it adopts once the percentage of adopters in its circle exceeds its threshold, or in society when its circle is empty. Every agent decides from the adoptions at the start of the step.

    BelieverElement(label, visual=VisualSpec('agents', {'color_by': 'caring', 'color_range': (0.0, 3.0)}), *, belief=0.0, caring=0.0, influence=1.0, learning=1.0)

    Bases: Element

    Agent of the belief diffusion model, with the weights it gives to four levels of caring about an issue.

    The levels are not caring at all, caring less, caring a bit, and caring passionately. An agent cares at the level of its largest weight. In influentials, a MapInteraction of the vector of weights adds every step learning times the influence of every neighbour to the weight of the level the neighbour cares at, a tie broken at random, and a second one sets the level of caring.

    ClubMemberElement(label, visual=OPINION_VISUAL, *, opinion=0.0, degree=0.0, beta=1.0, appeal=0.0, deciding=0.1, activity=0.5, rewiring=0.5)

    Bases: Element

    Agent that is in a club (spin 1) or out of it (spin 0), aligned by a heat bath with the agents it knows.

    Every step an agent reconsiders with probability deciding and then joins with probability 1 / (1 + exp(-2 β h)), where the field h counts the acquaintances in the club minus those out of it, plus the appeal of the club. The interaction SeparationInteraction dissolves acquaintances between a member and an outsider and reforms them towards an agent of the same side.

    CoevolvingVoterElement(label, visual=OPINION_VISUAL, *, opinion=0.0, degree=0.0, activity=0.5, rewiring=0.3)

    Bases: Element

    Voter of a coevolving network, with a binary opinion, its degree, and its number of discordant neighbours.

    ConfidenceCouplingInteraction(label, *, target, state='opinion')

    Bases: CouplingInteraction

    Hegselmann-Krause coupling, the move of every agent to the mean opinion of the neighbours within its confidence.

    Every step every agent takes at once the mean opinion of its neighbours, itself included, whose opinions differ from its own by less than its parameter confidence. It is the map form of coupling with the normalized mean over the neighbours of the network weighted by the indicator of the confidence.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    label str

    The label of the interaction, unique among the parts of its system.

    		 required
    target Annotated[str, RoleSpec()]

    The path of the group of agents, on the nodes of a network.

    		 required
    state str

    The name of the state that holds the opinions.

    		 'opinion'

    Examples:

>>> import numpy as np

    def act_on(self, members: list[AgentViewProtocol], model: ModelViewProtocol) -> None:
    """Convince the neighbours of the first member and a random neighbour of it when the two agree.

    Args:
        members:
            The persuader, followed by its neighbours.

        model:
            The model view of the rule.
    """
    agent, *neighbors = [member for member in members if isinstance(member, PersuaderView)]
    if not neighbors:
        return
    partner = neighbors[int(model.rng.integers(len(neighbors)))]
    if partner.opinion != agent.opinion:
        return
    for other in [*neighbors, *partner.neighbors()]:
        if isinstance(other, PersuaderView):
            other.opinion = agent.opinion

        width:
            The number of columns.

        height:

    )

    """Return the transition table of encounters between opinion levels, `A[h, k, i]`.

    A candidate at level `h` that meets a field particle at level `k` within `confidence` levels moves one level
    toward it with probability `persuasion`. One that meets a field particle further away moves one level away from it
    with probability `repulsion`, unless it holds an extreme opinion already.

    Args:
        levels:
            The number of opinion levels.

        confidence:
            The largest distance in levels at which encounters persuade.

        persuasion:
            The probability of moving toward a close opinion, a number or a parameter.

        repulsion:
            The probability of moving away from a distant opinion, a number or a parameter.

    Returns:
        table:
            The transition probabilities, which sum to one over the outcomes.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.social.opinion_dynamics import persuasion_table
        >>> persuasion_table(3, 1, 0.4, 0.1)[0, 1].astype(float).tolist()
        [0.6, 0.4, 0.0]
    """
    table = np.zeros((levels, levels, levels), dtype=object)
    for candidate in range(levels):
        for field in range(levels):
            toward = int(np.sign(field - candidate))
            outcome, probability = (
                (candidate + toward, persuasion)
                if abs(field - candidate) <= confidence
                else (candidate - toward, repulsion)
            )
            if toward and 0 <= outcome < levels:
                table[candidate, field, outcome] += probability
            table[candidate, field, candidate] += 1 - table[candidate, field].sum()
    return table
