    ExposureInteraction(label, *, target, rate=0.06, exponent=2.0)

    Bases: CouplingInteraction

    Infection of susceptible participants by the doses of their environments, and recovery, in discrete time.

    It is a CouplingInteraction over the hyperedges of a network in the map form. An environment, a hyperedge, gives each susceptible member the dose rate * rho ** exponent, where rho is the share of the other members that are infected, and the doses of the environments of a participant add up to D. In every step a susceptible participant is infected with probability 1 - exp(-D), and an infected one recovers with probability recovery, a parameter of the participants.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    label str

    The label of the interaction.

    target Annotated[str, RoleSpec()]

    The path of the participants on the network.

    		 required
    rate Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=closed_open(0, oo))]

    The dose of an environment whose other members are all infected.

    		 0.06
    exponent Annotated[float, ParameterSpec(domain=open(0, oo))]

    The exponent of the infection kernel, sublinear below 1 and supralinear above.

    		 2.0

    def write(self, model: ModelView) -> None:
    """Write the words of one step.

    Args:
        model:
            The model.
    """
    words = model.agents(self.target, GrowingWordView)
    if not words:
        return
    innovation = words[0].innovation
    counts = [int(word.count) for word in words]
    tokens = np.repeat(np.arange(len(counts)), counts).tolist()
    for _ in range(self.words_per_step):
        index = len(counts) if model.rng.random() < innovation else tokens[int(model.rng.integers(len(tokens)))]
        if index == len(counts):
            counts.append(0)
        counts[index] += 1
        tokens.append(index)
    for word, count in zip(words, counts, strict=False):
        word.count = float(count)
    for count in counts[len(words) :]:
        model.create(self.target, count=float(count))

    hypergraph_exposure_sis(n_nodes=1000, *, sizes=(10, 20, 30), hyperdegree=5.0, rate=0.06, exponent=2.0, recovery=0.05, share=0.3, seed=0)

    Return the SIS contagion of St-Onge et al. in the environments of a hypergraph, with a nonlinear kernel.

    Participants meet in environments, the hyperedges of a random hypergraph. In every step an environment gives a susceptible member the dose rate * rho ** exponent from the share rho of its other members that are infected, the member is infected with probability 1 - exp(-D) of the sum D of its doses, and an infected participant recovers with probability recovery. Heterogeneous exposure times give the power law rho ** exponent to the infection kernel. With a sublinear or linear kernel the epidemic sets in continuously, and with a supralinear kernel, exponent > 1, the healthy state stays stable: an endemic state coexists with it above a critical rate, so a small seed dies out and a large one persists. In mean field the prevalence I is endemic where k rate I ** (exponent - 1) (1 - I) = recovery for k environments per participant.

    Parameters:

    Name Type Description Default
    n_nodes int

    The number of participants.

    		 1000
    sizes Sequence[int]

    The sizes of the environments.

    		 (10, 20, 30)
    hyperdegree float

    The mean number of environments of a participant, split evenly over the sizes, whose members are drawn at random.

    		 5.0
    rate float

    The dose of an environment whose other members are all infected.

    		 0.06
    exponent float

    The exponent of the infection kernel.

    		 2.0
    recovery float

    The probability of recovery in a step.

    		 0.05
    share float

    The share of infected participants at the start.

    		 0.3
    seed int

    susceptible member the dose `rate * rho ** exponent` from the share `rho` of its other members that are infected,
    the member is infected with probability `1 - exp(-D)` of the sum `D` of its doses, and an infected participant
    recovers with probability `recovery`. Heterogeneous exposure times give the power law `rho ** exponent` to the
    infection kernel. With a sublinear or linear kernel the epidemic sets in continuously, and with a supralinear
    kernel, `exponent > 1`, the healthy state stays stable: an endemic state coexists with it above a critical rate,
    so a small seed dies out and a large one persists. In mean field the prevalence `I` is endemic where `k rate
    I ** (exponent - 1) (1 - I) = recovery` for `k` environments per participant.

    Args:
        n_nodes:
            The number of participants.

        sizes:
            The sizes of the environments.

        hyperdegree:
            The mean number of environments of a participant, split evenly over the sizes, whose members are drawn at
            random.

        rate:
            The dose of an environment whose other members are all infected.

        exponent:
            The exponent of the infection kernel.

        recovery:
            The probability of recovery in a step.

        share:
            The share of infected participants at the start.

        seed:
            The seed of the hypergraph and the infected participants.

    Returns:
        system:
            The model, with the states `participants.infected` and the prevalence `prevalence`.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.social.spreading import hypergraph_exposure_sis
        >>> hypergraph_exposure_sis(40).elements[0].check().prepare().size_
        40
    """
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    hypergraph = xgi.Hypergraph()
    hypergraph.add_nodes_from(range(n_nodes))
    for size in sizes:
        number = round(n_nodes * hyperdegree / (len(sizes) * size))
        hypergraph.add_edges_from(rng.choice(n_nodes, size, replace=False).tolist() for _ in range(number))
    infected = (rng.permutation(n_nodes) < round(share * n_nodes)).astype(float)
    participants = LatticeElement(
        'participants',
        element=ParticipantElement,
        space=NetworkSpace(xgi.to_graph(hypergraph), hyperedges=hypergraph),
        values={'infected': infected, 'recovery': recovery},
        visual=VisualSpec('sites', {'states': ['infected'], 'low': 0.0, 'high': 1.0}),
    )
    exposure = ExposureInteraction('exposure', target='participants', rate=rate, exponent=exponent)
    return System(elements=[participants], interactions=[exposure])

def simplicial_contagion(
    n_nodes: int = 200,
    *,
    infectivity: float = 0.75,
    triangle_infectivity: float = 3.0,
    mean_degree: float = 20.0,
    mean_triangles: float = 6.0,
    recovery: float = 1.0,
    share: float = 0.4,
    seed: int = 0,
) -> System:
    """Return the simplicial contagion of Iacopini et al., an SIS process on the links and triangles of a complex.

    A random simplicial complex joins every pair of nodes with probability `p1` and fills every triple with probability
    `p2`, chosen so that a node has `mean_degree` links and `mean_triangles` triangles on average. A susceptible node is
    infected at rate `beta = infectivity * recovery / <k>` by every infected neighbour, and at rate `beta_triangle =
    triangle_infectivity * recovery / <k_triangle>` by every triangle whose two other members are infected, and an
    infected node recovers at rate `recovery`. The mean-field prevalence follows `rho' = -rho + infectivity rho (1 -
    rho) + triangle_infectivity rho^2 (1 - rho)` in units of the recovery time. For `triangle_infectivity > 1` and
    infectivities below the epidemic threshold 1, an endemic state coexists with the healthy one, so the epidemic
    dies out from a small seed and persists from a large one.

    Args:
        n_nodes:
            The number of nodes.

        infectivity:
            The rescaled rate of infection through links.

        triangle_infectivity:
            The rescaled rate of infection through triangles.

        mean_degree:
            The mean number of links of a node, which sets `p1` together with the sides of the triangles.

        mean_triangles:
            The mean number of triangles of a node, which sets `p2`.

        recovery:
            The rate of recovery.

        share:
            The share of infected nodes at the start.

        seed:
            The seed of the complex and the infected nodes.

    Returns:
        system:
            The model, with the states `nodes.infected` and the prevalence `prevalence`.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.social.spreading import simplicial_contagion
        >>> system = simplicial_contagion(30, mean_degree=6.0, mean_triangles=2.0)
        >>> system.elements[0].check().prepare().size_
        30
    """
    pairs = math.comb(n_nodes - 1, PAIR - 1)
    triples = math.comb(n_nodes - 1, TRIANGLE - 1)
    triangles = min(mean_triangles / triples, 1.0)
    links = min(max(mean_degree - 2 * mean_triangles, 0.0) / pairs, 1.0)
    graph, hyperedges = _simplicial_complex(n_nodes, links, triangles, seed)
    sizes = [len(members) for members in hyperedges]
    degree = max(PAIR * sizes.count(PAIR) / n_nodes, 1.0)
    shared = max(TRIANGLE * sizes.count(TRIANGLE) / n_nodes, 1.0)
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed + 1)
    infected = (rng.permutation(n_nodes) < round(share * n_nodes)).astype(float)
    nodes = LatticeElement(
        'nodes',
        element=InfectiveElement,
        space=NetworkSpace(graph, hyperedges=hyperedges),
        values={'infected': infected, 'recovery': recovery},
        visual=VisualSpec('sites', {'states': ['infected'], 'low': 0.0, 'high': 1.0}),
    )
    infection = SimplicialInfectionInteraction(
        'infection',
        target='nodes',
        beta=infectivity * recovery / degree,
        beta_triangle=triangle_infectivity * recovery / shared,
    )
    return System(elements=[nodes], interactions=[infection])
