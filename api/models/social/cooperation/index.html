    PrisonersDilemmaInteraction(label, *, target, imitation='best')

    Bases: LocalRuleInteraction

    Play of the weak prisoner's dilemma between neighbours, and imitation of more successful neighbours.

    It is a stochastic LocalRuleInteraction of the strategies over the 5 by 5 square around a player, a cooperator at 1 and a defector at 0. Every step each player plays the weak dilemma, with reward 1, temptation temptation, and 0 otherwise, against itself and its eight neighbours. It then copies the best player among them, or a random neighbour with the Fermi probability 1 / (1 + exp((P_own - P_other) / noise)). The temptation and the noise are parameters of the players.

    SimplicialDilemmaInteraction(label, *, target, sucker=-0.2, temptation=1.2, sucker_allied=0.5, temptation_allied=0.8, sucker_opposed=-0.2, temptation_opposed=1.2, phase=None)

    Bases: CouplingInteraction

    Play of two-player games along the links of the triangles of a simplicial complex, which sets the payoffs.

    It is a CouplingInteraction over the hyperedges of a network in the map form, a cooperator at 1 and a defector at 0. Every game pays 1 for mutual cooperation, 0 for mutual defection, and the sucker's payoff S and the temptation T when the strategies differ. A triangle of three links is three hyperedges of two members, along which the players play game 1 of S1 and T1. A triangle filled as a 2-simplex is one hyperedge of three members, where a player plays each other member while the third looks on: game 2 of S2 and T2 when the third member shares the player's strategy, and game 3 of S3 and T3 otherwise. The payoff of a player is the sum over the two links of every triangle around it divided by twice its number of triangles, a parameter of the players.

    def act_on(self, members: list[AgentViewProtocol], model: ModelViewProtocol) -> None:
    """Set the fitness of the first member to its mean score per round against the two others.

    Args:
        members:
            The agent, followed by its two neighbours in the ring.

        model:
            The model view of the rule.
    """
    agent, *opponents = [member for member in members if isinstance(member, StrategistView)]
    total = sum(play(agent.genome, opponent.genome, self.rounds)[0] for opponent in opponents)
    agent.fitness = total / (PAIR * self.rounds)

    hypergraph_public_goods(n_nodes=400, *, sizes=(3, 5), hyperdegree=2.0, synergy=4.0, noise=0.5, share=0.5, seed=0)

    Return the public goods game on a hypergraph, played in every hyperedge, with Fermi imitation.

    Every hyperedge is a group that plays the public goods game: a cooperator pays 1, and the pot multiplied by synergy is shared by the members. Every step has two phases. In the phase play every player takes the sum of its shares minus its contributions over the hyperedges around it, and in the phase imitate every player copies a random neighbour, a member of one of its hyperedges, with the Fermi probability 1 / (1 + exp((P_own - P_other) / K)) of the payoffs of the same step, at the noise K. In a well-mixed population defection wins in a group of m for synergy < m, so cooperation needs a synergy near the sizes of the hyperedges.

    The number of players.

    		 400
    sizes Sequence[int]

    		 (3, 5)
    hyperdegree float

    The mean number of hyperedges of every size around a player, whose members are drawn at random.

    		 2.0
    synergy float

    		 4.0
    noise float

    		 0.5

def hypergraph_public_goods(
    n_nodes: int = 400,
    *,
    sizes: Sequence[int] = (3, 5),
    hyperdegree: float = 2.0,
    synergy: float = 4.0,
    noise: float = 0.5,
    share: float = 0.5,
    seed: int = 0,
) -> System:
    """Return the public goods game on a hypergraph, played in every hyperedge, with Fermi imitation.

    Every hyperedge is a group that plays the public goods game: a cooperator pays 1, and the pot multiplied by
    `synergy` is shared by the members. Every step has two phases. In the phase `play` every player takes the sum of
    its shares minus its contributions over the hyperedges around it, and in the phase `imitate` every player copies a
    random neighbour, a member of one of its hyperedges, with the Fermi probability `1 / (1 + exp((P_own - P_other) /
    K))` of the payoffs of the same step, at the noise `K`. In a well-mixed population defection wins in a group of `m`
    for `synergy < m`, so cooperation needs a synergy near the sizes of the hyperedges.

    Args:
        n_nodes:
            The number of players.

        sizes:
            The sizes of the hyperedges.

        hyperdegree:
            The mean number of hyperedges of every size around a player, whose members are drawn at random.

        synergy:
            The factor that multiplies the contributions of a hyperedge.

        noise:
            The noise of the Fermi imitation.

        share:
            The share of cooperators at the start.

        seed:
            The seed of the hypergraph and the initial strategies.

    Returns:
        system:
            The model, with the strategies `players.strategy` and the payoffs `players.payoff`.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.social.cooperation import hypergraph_public_goods
        >>> hypergraph_public_goods(30).elements[0].check().prepare().size_
        30
    """
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    hypergraph = _hypergraph(n_nodes, sizes, hyperdegree, rng)
    graph = xgi.to_graph(hypergraph)
    strategies = (rng.random(n_nodes) < share).astype(float)
    players = LatticeElement(
        'players',
        element=ContributorElement,
        space=NetworkSpace(graph, hyperedges=hypergraph),
        values={'strategy': strategies, 'synergy': synergy, 'noise': noise},
        visual=VisualSpec('sites', {'states': ['strategy'], 'low': 0.0, 'high': 1.0}),
    )
    imitation = ImitationInteraction(
        'imitation',
        target='players',
        state='strategy',
        rule='fermi',
        form='probability',
        intensity=1 / noise,
        phase='imitate',
    )
    return System(
        elements=[players],
        interactions=[HyperedgeGoodsInteraction('play', target='players', phase='play'), imitation],
        time=DiscreteTime(schedule=PhasesScheduleSpec(tuple(PhaseSpec(phase) for phase in PHASES))),
    )

        scores:
            The total scores of the two players.

    Examples:
        >>> from skcomplex.models.social.cooperation import play
        >>> play([1] * 7, [0] * 7)
        (0.0, 30.0)

    moves: tuple[list[int], list[int]] = ([], [])
    scores = [0.0, 0.0]

        move = (int(genomes[0][_gene(moves[1])]), int(genomes[1][_gene(moves[0])]))

        moves[0].append(move[0])
        moves[1].append(move[1])
    return scores[0], scores[1]

        'imitation',

        state='strategy',
        rule='fermi',
        form='probability',
        intensity=1 / noise,
        phase='imitate',
