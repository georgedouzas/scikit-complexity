    AnisotropicBrownianParticlesSystem(n_particles=200, *, D_par=1.0, D_perp=1.0, D_r=0.75, D_spin=0.75, D_c=0.0, random_state=0, label='system')

    Bases: BaseSystem

    Overdamped axisymmetric Brownian particles in three dimensions.

    The particles translate and rotate.

    Every particle diffuses with D_par along its axis u and with D_perp across it, its axis diffuses on the unit sphere with D_r, so that <u(t) . u(0)> = exp(-2 D_r t), and it spins about its axis with D_spin. The defaults are a Janus sphere of unit radius, D_par = D_perp = D_t and D_r = 3 D_t / 4, whose orientation is visible although its diffusion is isotropic. A prolate ellipsoid has D_par > D_perp from Perrin's factors, so its displacement over times shorter than 1 / D_r is elongated along its axis. A chiral body, such as a spiral, has a coupling D_c of the translation along its axis and the rotation about it, <dr_par dpsi> = 2 D_c dt. These are Challenges 5.1 to 5.3 of Argun et al. The axis is held as a unit vector and rescaled to unit length after every step, and the particles start at the origin with axes drawn uniformly on the sphere. The system has the positions particles.x, particles.y, particles.z, the axes particles.ux, particles.uy, particles.uz, and the spins particles.psi.

    BiasedTrapWalkSystem(n_walkers=100, *, n_sites=300, alpha=0.5, tau0=1.0, random_state=0, label='system')

    Bases: BaseSystem

    Walkers that hop always to the right through quenched traps.

    The trapping times are broadly distributed.

    Every walker moves along its own line of sites and stays tau_i steps on site i before it hops to site i + 1.     BrownianNearWallSystem(z=1.0, *, D0=1.0, a=1.0, drift=0.2, convention='anti-ito', label='system')

    A Brownian particle that settles on a wall.

    Its diffusion slows near the wall, D(z) = D0 z² / (z² + a²).

The laws are symmetric about the wall at z = 0, which reflects the particle at every frame. The system has the distance particle.z.

    TrappedSphereElement(label, visual=SPHERES, *, x=0.0, center=0.0)

    Bases: Element

    Colloidal sphere at the position x, held by an optical trap.

    The trap is centred at center.

    The sphere has no friction of its own. A mobility interaction moves it through the fluid it shares with the other spheres, and its trap is one of the forces the mobility turns into velocities.
