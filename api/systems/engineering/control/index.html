    BangBangPlantElement(label, visual=None, *, x=0.0, y=0.0, u=1.0, a=0.0)

    Bases: Element

    Second-order linear plant under a bang-bang control.

    The control is held between samples, and it steers the plant to the origin. The plant follows x' = y and y' = a x + u with the control u of size 1, a = 0 for a free particle, a = -1 for a harmonic oscillator, and a = 1 for a saddle. The control stays constant between samples, and at every sample takes -1 above the switching curve y = g(x) of the plant and 1 below it. The curve is made of the two trajectories of the plant under u = 1 and u = -1 that reach the origin: g(x) = -sign(x) sqrt(2 |x|) for the free particle, the half circles g(x) = sqrt(1 - (x + 1)^2) on [-2, 0] and -sqrt(1 - (x - 1)^2) on [0, 2], and 0 outside, for the harmonic oscillator, and g(x) = sqrt(x^2 - 2 x) for x <= 0 and -sqrt(x^2 + 2 x) for x >= 0 for the saddle.

    Bases: BaseSystem

    A plant that a discrete-time controller steers with model predictive control.

    The control runs on a fitted surrogate. The system runs the plant under a random binary input at the limits of its range, drawn every sample, and fits mode_decomposition with the input to the runs.     A room heated by a furnace under a thermostat while heat leaks to the outside.

    The furnace adds the gap between the setting and the room temperature, at most furnace degrees per hour, and nothing when the room is warmer than the setting. The room loses leak of its difference to the outside temperature per hour, so it settles below the setting, at (setting + leak * outside) / (1 + leak) for a constant outside. The outside temperature swings by swing around outside over a day, warmest at noon. The system has the room temperature room.T in hours.

    Parameters:

    The thermostat setting.

    		 0.1

    The mean outside temperature.

    		 10.0
    swing float

    The amplitude of the daily swing of the outside temperature.

    		 0.0
    label str

    The label of the system, which names it when it is nested in another one.

    		 'system'
