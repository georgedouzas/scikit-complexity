    CellComplexSpace(space, *, faces=None, dimension=LOWEST_DIMENSION, label='space')

    Bases: NetworkSpace

    Network of the cells of a lattice or a network, with their oriented incidence.

    The incidence is between consecutive degrees. Its nodes are the sites or nodes of the space it derives from, its edges the oriented links or edges, and its hyperedges the oriented plaquettes or faces in cyclic node order. Every group, binding, and neighbourhood of a network space applies to it, so groups on its edges and hyperedges hold states on links and plaquettes. It has no layers.

    The links of a lattice are numbered by direction and then by site, and point along the positive direction of their axis. The plaquettes are numbered by plane and then by site, with planes (0, 1), (0, 2), ... in increasing order, and turn from the first axis of the plane to the second. Fixed and reflecting edges drop the cells that leave the box. A face of a network is oriented by its node cycle. The faces of a clique complex and the triangles of simplicial hyperedges take their incidence from xgi.linalg.hodge_matrix.boundary_matrix, and the other faces from the sum of the oriented edges around them.
