# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Lets a user run cellular automata over finitely generated groups, given by finite presentations, Mealy automata, or
permutation or matrix generators, decide their injectivity and surjectivity on finite universes, study linear automata
and subshifts, and measure their information content.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Automata run on Cayley graphs of groups
Local rules SHALL run on Cayley graphs of finitely presented groups, built on finite quotients or on finite balls, with
memory sets given as group elements.

#### Scenario: Free group majority
- **WHEN** the majority automaton on the free group with two generators runs on a ball of radius 6
- **THEN** every interior cell takes the majority value of itself and its four neighbours given by the generators and
  their inverses

#### Scenario: Orientation is kept
- **WHEN** a rule copies the value at the generator `a` on the Heisenberg group
- **THEN** each cell takes the value of `g a`, not of `a g`

### Requirement: Groups are given by other presentations
Group spaces SHALL be built from automaton groups given by a Mealy automaton as a wreath recursion, and from permutation
or matrix groups given by generators. Their balls SHALL hold one site per distinct element, decided by the word problem
of the presentation, not by relators.

#### Scenario: Grigorchuk growth
- **WHEN** a user builds group spaces on balls of the Grigorchuk group with the generators `a, b, c, d` and radii 0 to 5
- **THEN** the balls hold 1, 5, 11, 23, 40, and 68 sites, and every site is a distinct element of the group

#### Scenario: Free matrix group
- **WHEN** a user builds balls of radii 0 to 3 of the matrix group generated by `[[1, 2], [0, 1]]` and
  `[[1, 0], [2, 1]]` over the integers
- **THEN** the balls hold 1, 5, 17, and 53 sites, as in the free group of rank 2

#### Scenario: Finite permutation group
- **WHEN** a user builds balls of the symmetric group on three points generated by `(0 1)` and `(0 1 2)`
- **THEN** the ball of radius 1 holds 4 sites, and the balls of radius 2 and more hold all 6 elements

#### Scenario: Missing extra
- **WHEN** a user builds a group space of an automaton group without the `groups` extra installed
- **THEN** an `ImportError` is raised whose message names the `groups` extra and how to install it

#### Scenario: Rules run without GAP
- **WHEN** a rule runs on a built group space for many steps
- **THEN** each step reads the neighbour table on the NumPy or Numba backend and makes no call to GAP

### Requirement: Automaton groups give level quotients
Automaton groups SHALL give finite quotients by their action on a level of the tree, on which configurations are
periodic and the theory checks apply.

#### Scenario: Grigorchuk level quotients
- **WHEN** a user builds group spaces on the quotients of the Grigorchuk group by its action on levels 1 to 4 of the
  binary tree
- **THEN** they hold 2, 8, 128, and 4096 sites, and a rule runs on them with periodic configurations

### Requirement: Group spaces stream as Cayley graphs
Group spaces SHALL stream to Rerun as graph nodes and edges, with one node per site, one edge per generator, a layout
computed once, and node colours from the cell states at each step.

#### Scenario: Grigorchuk ball in the viewer
- **WHEN** the majority automaton on a ball of radius 8 of the Grigorchuk group is logged
- **THEN** the viewer shows 271 nodes on rings by word length, joined by the generator edges, and their colours change
  with the states over time

### Requirement: Injectivity and surjectivity are decided on finite universes
The analysis layer SHALL decide injectivity, surjectivity, and pre-injectivity of an automaton on finite quotients and
windows, find Garden of Eden patterns, and find the inverse of a reversible automaton.

#### Scenario: Life has a Garden of Eden
- **WHEN** a user checks the Game of Life on a 6 × 6 torus
- **THEN** it is reported not surjective, with a configuration that has no preimage

#### Scenario: Reversible rule
- **WHEN** a user asks for the inverse of the elementary shift automaton
- **THEN** the result is the shift in the opposite direction, and their composition is the identity

### Requirement: Linear automata are decided algebraically
Linear automata SHALL be represented as group-ring elements whose invertibility and surjectivity are decided
algebraically.

#### Scenario: Laplacian over a finite field
- **WHEN** a user checks the discrete Laplacian on the cycle of length 5 over the field with 2 elements
- **THEN** the answer matches the invertibility of the corresponding circulant matrix

### Requirement: Subshifts constrain configurations
Subshifts of finite type SHALL check configurations against forbidden patterns, count admissible patterns, and sample
admissible configurations.

#### Scenario: Golden mean shift
- **WHEN** a user counts admissible words of length n in the subshift that forbids `11`
- **THEN** the counts follow the Fibonacci numbers, and the entropy estimate approaches the log of the golden ratio

### Requirement: Automata information is measured
The analysis layer SHALL estimate the entropy of configuration sets along windows, track the spatial entropy rate of
runs, and compute difference patterns of single-cell perturbations.

#### Scenario: Entropy decreases
- **WHEN** an elementary automaton runs from a random configuration on a large ring
- **THEN** the spatial entropy rate never increases from one step to the next beyond estimation error
